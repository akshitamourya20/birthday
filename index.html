A Heartfelt Tribute

To Mummy, My First Teacher & Eternal Guru 🌸

A letter written straight from the depths of my heart

❤️

My Dearest Mummy,

They say that God could not be everywhere, and therefore He created Mothers. But for me, He blessed me twice over — because in you, He gave me not just the most loving mother, but also my first teacher, my truest philosopher, and my eternal Guru.

How poetic and beautiful it is that your birthday arrives hand-in-hand with Teacher's Day! Every single day, you step into school and pour your wisdom, patience, and love into shaping so many young lives. But long before I ever held a schoolbook, you held my tiny hands and taught me how to speak, how to walk, how to be honest, and how to face the world with courage and kindness.

“ A teacher inspires the mind, but a mother touches the soul. You do both effortlessly every single day. ”

Thank you for every silent sacrifice you’ve made, every prayer you’ve whispered for my happiness, every delicious meal made with love, and every warm hug that instantly makes all worries vanish. Your strength inspires me, your smile illuminates our entire house, and your blessings are my greatest superpower.

On this double celebration of your life and your noble calling, I pray for your lifelong health, sparkling joy, endless laughter, and boundless peace. May every dream in your pure heart come true!

With endless love, respect, and gratitude,

Your Loving Child Always 💕
